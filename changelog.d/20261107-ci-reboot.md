### Build — Stricter CI and per-class coverage gates

The build now enforces per-class JaCoCo coverage (95% line, 90% branch, with measured per-class overrides and an 80% bundle backstop), runs Checkstyle first, and uses Boot's own Mockito 5 and JUnit launcher instead of the old pins. CI is one `Build, Test & Coverage` job plus a changed-lines coverage check on PRs; mutation testing and the live-API tests run on a weekly schedule, and green minor/patch Dependabot PRs are merged automatically. The OWASP scan stays out of CI until a valid `NVD_API_KEY` is set (`./mvnw dependency-check:check` still works locally).
